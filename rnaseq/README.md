# Reference-based RNA-seq in Galaxy

## Overview

I used the Galaxy Training Network reference-based RNA-seq tutorial as a guided analysis of a small *Drosophila melanogaster* dataset. The central comparison was **treat versus untreat**, with sequencing layout included as a second factor because the supplied count files contained both single-end and paired-end samples.

The completed downstream analysis produced DESeq2 differential-expression results, normalized counts, GO enrichment with goseq, two significant KEGG pathway rows, Pathview pathway images, and a heatmap. The upstream mapping branch was not completed successfully: STAR mapping failed, so I did not report mapping rates or present the planned STAR-to-featureCounts branch as a successful result.

## What This Case Study Shows

- construction and checking of Galaxy dataset collections;
- handling of treatment and sequencing-layout covariates;
- diagnosis of a dataset-tagging error before DESeq2;
- interpretation of differential-expression and enrichment outputs;
- separation of completed results from failed workflow steps.

## Evidence Boundary

The downstream DESeq2 analysis used seven GTN-provided featureCounts files. These were training inputs supplied by the tutorial, not counts generated by the failed STAR jobs in my own Galaxy history. Raw reads, private Galaxy screenshots, and large intermediate files are intentionally excluded from this public repository.
