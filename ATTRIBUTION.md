# Attribution and Provenance

## Galaxy Training Network Workflow

The workflow in `rnaseq/workflow/qc-mapping-counting-paired-and-single.ga` is an official Galaxy Training Network (GTN) workflow for the tutorial **Reference-based RNA-Seq data analysis**. I used it as a training workflow and do not claim authorship of its design.

- Tutorial: <https://training.galaxyproject.org/training-material/topics/transcriptomics/tutorials/ref-based/tutorial.html>
- Persistent tutorial link: <https://gxy.io/GTN:T00295>
- Workflow license: MIT, as recorded in the exported workflow metadata.
- Workflow creator and ORCID metadata are preserved inside the `.ga` file.
- The accompanying [MIT notice](rnaseq/workflow/LICENSE-MIT.txt) reproduces the upstream copyright and license from [GTN LICENSE.md](https://github.com/galaxyproject/training-material/blob/main/LICENSE.md).

## Analysis Tools

Galaxy, DESeq2, goseq, KEGG/Pathview, MEGA12, IQ-TREE, STAR, featureCounts, Cutadapt, MultiQC, and Falco are third-party tools or services. Their names are used only to document the analysis methods and remain subject to their respective licenses and citation requirements.

## Public Data

The RNA-seq sample accession identifiers and protein database accessions cited in the analysis refer to public biological data. Raw sequence files are not redistributed in this repository.
